XS-Leaks oracle simulator

An XS-Leak doesn't steal data directly — it asks a logged-in victim's browser a yes/no question about another site and reads the answer through a side channel the Same-Origin Policy never covered. Here the secret is: "Is the visitor an admin on target.example?" Pick an oracle, then toggle the target's defenses and watch the side channel open or close. Everything is modeled — nothing is fetched.
